Privacy Notice

Last updated: 15 October 2026

About This Integration

Music Assistant Alexa is a personal, non-commercial integration intended to connect Amazon Alexa devices with a privately hosted Music Assistant server.

Information Used

The integration uses Amazon authentication to authorise access to the connected Amazon account and enable music playback functionality.

How Information Is Used

Authentication information is used solely to operate the personal Alexa and Music Assistant integration. The integration is not intended for public or commercial use.

Data Storage

Authentication credentials and integration configuration may be stored on the owner's self-hosted systems to maintain connectivity.

Third-Party Services

Amazon Alexa and Login with Amazon are used for authentication and device functionality. Their respective privacy policies also apply.

Data Removal

The owner can disconnect the integration, revoke its Amazon authorisation, and delete locally stored integration credentials.
